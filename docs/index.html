Fllr

Filler content, one click away. A tiny macOS menu bar app that puts placeholder text, images, people and files on your clipboard.

Build it yourself View on GitHub

Three steps, no window

The interface shows shapes rather than labels. Anything extra appears only when you hover.

  1. OpenClick the Fllr mark in your menu bar.
  2. HoverThe top line tells you what a click will copy, such as “Copy 3 sentences”.
  3. ClickIt is on your clipboard. The popover closes and a notification confirms it.

Four fillrs

Each fillr is a tab. You choose in the settings which ones you want; Text and Images are on by default.

Text

Lorem ipsum as 1–5 words, 1–5 sentences or 1–4 paragraphs.

  • Click copies plain text.
  • ⌥-click wraps it in <p> tags.

Images

Placeholder PNGs at the exact pixel size, in sizes you define yourself.

  • Click copies the image.
  • Drag it straight into Figma, Keynote or Finder.
  • ⌥-click copies an <img> tag with matching width and height.

People optional

A fictional person with a name, email, phone number, address, date of birth and IBAN, in the format of the country that matches your language.

  • Click a field to copy it.
  • ⌥-click the copy tile for the whole person as JSON.

Files optional

Dummy TXT, CSV, JSON and PDF files of 1 KB, 100 KB, 1 MB or 10 MB, for testing uploads and limits.

  • Click copies the file, ready to paste.
  • Drag it into an upload field or folder.

Settings

Everything else stays out of sight, behind the gear.

  • Fillrs: tick the tabs you want.
  • Language: English, Deutsch, Français or Nederlands.
  • Image sizes: edit, add or remove sizes in pixels.

Where the content comes from

Everything is generated on your Mac. Fllr makes no network requests and collects nothing.

Text

Classic lorem ipsum. It always opens with “Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet”; the rest is drawn at random from a pool of Latin words built into the app.

Images

Drawn locally as a grey rectangle with its dimensions. Only the optional <img> tag points to placehold.co.

People

Invented from short lists of common names, streets and cities for the UK, Germany, France and the Netherlands. Emails end in @example.com; IBANs pass checksum validation but use bank codes that do not exist.

Files

TXT and PDF are exactly the requested size. CSV and JSON stay just under it so they remain valid, and are filled with the same fictional people.

Icons

Phosphor Icons in Bold and Fill, under the MIT licence. The menu bar mark is Fllr’s own.

Build it yourself

There is no download yet. Building takes about a minute and needs macOS 14 or later with the Xcode Command Line Tools; full Xcode is not required.

git clone https://github.com/WillMuijrers/Fllr.git
cd Fllr
./build.sh --run

On first launch macOS asks whether Fllr may send notifications. Allow it to get the “copied” confirmation.
